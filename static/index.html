    How to play IB-Flip

    Get rid of all your cards: your hand, then your face-up cards, then your face-down cards. You must finish legally.

    A round

    1. Deal. Everyone gets 3 face-down cards, 3 face-up cards on top of them, and a hand of 3.
    2. Set up. Swap hand cards with your face-up cards, or combine matching ranks from your hand and face-up slots. All three face-up slots must hold a card before you are ready. Confirmed plays are final.
    3. Lowest card starts. From lowest to highest: 3, 6, 8, 9, J, Q, K, A, 7, 2, 10, 4, 5.
    4. Play one or more cards of the same rank onto the live pile. Draw back up to 3 while the deck lasts. If you can't play, you may flip the top card of the deck; it plays if it fits.
    5. When the deck is empty and you can't play, pick up the live pile. When your hand is empty, play your face-up cards, then your face-down cards without looking.
    6. The first player to finish legally wins. If your last play would give you another turn (a 2, a 10, an 8 with two players left, or flipping any set except 4s and 9s), you lose. Finishing by flipping 4s is fine, but if the discard pile has a 3 you take it and stay in. Finishing by flipping 9s loses unless the new top card is an 8.

    Card powers

    Flips

    A 10, or four of a kind in a row, flips the live pile to the discard, and the player who flipped goes again. Four of a kind has an extra effect for these ranks:

    Four 3s
    Deal one 3 to each other player.
    Four 4s
    Take a 3 from the discard. Then it is the next player's turn.
    Four 8s
    The player who flipped loses.
    Four 9s
    The discard pile becomes the new live pile.
