    How to play IB-Flip

    Get rid of all your cards — hand, then face-up, then face-down — and finish legally.

    1. Deal. Everyone gets 3 face-down, 3 face-up on top, and a 3-card hand.
    2. Fix your hand. Swap hand cards with your face-up cards, or stack matching ranks.
    3. Lowest card starts (3 is lowest; then 6, 8, 9, J, Q, K, A, 7, 2, 10, 4, 5).
    4. Play one or more cards of the same rank onto the live pile. Draw back to 3 while the deck lasts. Stuck? Flip the top of the deck and hope.
    5. Deck empty: can't play → pick up the live pile. Hand empty → play your face-up cards, then face-down cards blind.
    6. First to finish legally wins. Finishing in a way that gives you another turn (on a 2, a 10, a flip, an 8 heads-up…) means you lose.

    Card powers

    Flips

    A 10 or four of a kind in a row flips the live pile to the discard and the flipper goes again. Special flips: 3s → deal one 3 to each other player · 4s → take a 3 from the discard, next player's turn · 8s → the flipper loses · 9s → the discard pile becomes the new live pile.

    Keyboard

    1–9 play the nth playable card · Space main action (Done / Pick up / Flip deck / Ready) · H hint · Esc close
